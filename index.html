Indian Penal Code (IPC) Sections for Women

Section 354 IPC

Assault or criminal force to woman with intent to outrage her modesty

Punishment: Up to 2 years imprisonment, or fine, or both

Section 354A IPC

Sexual harassment and punishment for sexual harassment

Punishment: Up to 3 years imprisonment, or fine, or both

Section 354B IPC

Assault or use of criminal force to woman with intent to disrobe

Punishment: 3 to 7 years imprisonment

Section 354C IPC

Voyeurism (watching or capturing images of a woman without consent)

Punishment: 3 to 7 years imprisonment + fine

Section 354D IPC

Stalking (following or contacting a woman despite clear disinterest)

Punishment: Up to 3 years imprisonment + fine

Section 509 IPC

Word, gesture or act intended to insult the modesty of a woman

Punishment: 1 year imprisonment, or fine, or both

Safety Awareness

Self Defense Tips

  • Always be aware of your surroundings
  • Trust your instincts - if something feels wrong, it probably is
  • Learn basic self-defense techniques (target eyes, nose, throat, knees, groin)
  • Carry a whistle or personal alarm

Digital Safety

  • Never share live location on social media
  • Use strong, unique passwords for all accounts
  • Be cautious with dating apps - meet in public places
  • Enable two-factor authentication where possible
